Self-reflection questions about the body, and finding the one question worth asking yourself.

The body is too much ground for one good question: a boss who wants your training mornings, a wrist still healing, a morning with energy to spare, one more episode past midnight, a lake too cold at dawn. So a list of questions about all of it stays general. Add a second topic, and combined they get you closer to the question you were looking for, even if you did not know it yet.

Pick two topics

The body
Health, energy, appetite, and the body you live in
Self and change
Identity, change, starting out or starting over, and who you are becoming

Together, they hold situations like:

  • A body that is learning, or changing
  • learning a skill as an adult
  • old gear that fits differently now
  • what you keep as your body changes

In Mind Wandern, a Path is a short series of self-reflection questions that starts somewhere in that combined ground.

In the app you can add more topics, or keep one away. For example:

  • + Desire and longing → find situations about what you want your body to do again
  • − Desire and longing → keep wanting away, and focus the situations on what your body is asking for now

  • Why not just a list of questions about the body?

    A list might ask

    How do you feel in your body today? What does your body need from you?

    Lists like that are written for anyone, about any body. They are a fine start, and if a list and a notebook are all you need, that is a good way to reflect.

    But when your body is on your mind, it is usually tied to something else: a friend’s pace, a job that needs your hands, a hobby you left behind. Starting there gets you to a question you can actually answer.
