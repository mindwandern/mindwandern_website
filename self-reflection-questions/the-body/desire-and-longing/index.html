Where the body meets desire

The body
Health, energy, appetite, and the body you live in
Desire and longing
Desire, longing, love, play, and what you want but cannot name

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Wanting back what you once loved doing
  • a craft you left years ago
  • an invitation into cold water
  • a Saturday morning just for you

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Those are example Paths, shortened for this page.

The Path
the shelf at the studio
The studio manager says your old shelf is still there, your half-finished bowl on it. You stand in the doorway, deciding whether to sign up for a Saturday morning at the wheel.

A question on the way

Now that a worry has eased, what do you want to begin again?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What would it be like to walk back into the studio and find your shelf exactly as you left it?”

Next on the way

Which part of your week would a Saturday morning at the wheel give up?

Further up this Path

Once the Saturday is booked, what will you tell yourself you are doing there, just for you?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What draws you most to the wheel: the wet clay or the quiet of a Saturday morning?”

Next on the way

When you picture your hands on the clay, what do you hope it will give back to you?

Further up this Path

If nobody else would ever see what you made, would you still want to sit down at the wheel?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What is the smallest thing that would need to be true for you to say yes to a Saturday at the wheel?”

Next on the way

Which of the things you have been putting off would you be glad to have started by next spring?

Further up this Path

Which small step toward that Saturday could you take before the week is out?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

Some invitations come from the water. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the dock at dawn
At dawn your friend is already wading into the lake, waving you toward the cold water. You stand on the dock with your towel.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What pulls you toward the cold water each morning, more than the swim itself?”

Next on the way

Standing on the dock, what small thing do you keep weighing before you step in?

Further up this Path

If the morning swim asked nothing of your body, whose invitation would you still answer?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When did you last notice your body asking for something gentler than before?”

Next on the way

When your friend is already in the water, what do you tell yourself about why you are still on the edge?

Further up this Path

When you decide whether the swim is worth it, who or what is actually doing the deciding?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would it change to say the cold water is not for you anymore?”

Next on the way

If the morning swim were taken away, what would you miss first?

Further up this Path

Whose voice would you still hear calling you in, if your friend stopped asking?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.
