Where the body meets work

The body
Health, energy, appetite, and the body you live in
Work and money
Work, money, and what you earn, keep, or lose

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Work hours and the time your body needs
  • a shift that takes your training mornings
  • going back to work while still recovering
  • the rest you take on purpose

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

The Path
the Saturday shift
Your long Sunday run is getting easier, and your supervisor has asked you to cover Saturday mornings for the rest of the season, just when your training needs them most.

A question on the way

What does your training need from you that no one at work will ever see?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When your boss asked for your Saturdays, what did you feel first?”

Next on the way

When your boss asked for more of your time, what did you notice in your body before you answered?

Further up this Path

What would you tell your boss about your Saturdays, if you knew they would actually hear it?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would you be giving up on Saturdays if you said yes?”

Next on the way

Where does the hope of your first race sit against the hours work is asking for?

Further up this Path

What is one thing you could do this week to protect the training you have already built?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When did you last feel your training and your job pull in opposite directions?”

Next on the way

When a Friday night runs short, what do you carry into Saturday morning?

Further up this Path

If you had to choose between the race and the extra pay, which would you be willing to lose?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

Some weeks your strength is still on its way back. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the slow return
Your strength is coming back a little each week. A work trip request is still in your inbox, and someone at home still needs a ride to their appointments.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “Which day of your week does work pull hardest against your need to rest?”

Next on the way

On your hardest day, what do you need before the work begins?

Further up this Path

Which kind of rest, taken on purpose, has actually helped you lately?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who at home is waiting on your energy?”

Next on the way

When your energy comes back a little, what part of your day do you notice first?

Further up this Path

Of the work you feel you must carry, which piece could wait if your body asked it to?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When you look at the work trip request, what do you already think the answer is?”

Next on the way

Which part of an unanswered request keeps coming back to you, even when you are not trying to decide?

Further up this Path

If your strength never returns to what it was, what would a full return to work look like for you?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.
