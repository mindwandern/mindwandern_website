The body + Attention and time

Self-reflection questions about late nights, good mornings, and where your energy goes.

One more episode, then it is past midnight and your legs are stiff on the couch. A morning with more energy than you have had since winter, and the bike, the closet and your mother’s Sunday lunch all waiting for the same few hours. Your body often notices where the hours went before you do. Start where The body meets Attention and time, and pick the prompt that speaks to you more.

Where the body meets attention and time

The body
Health, energy, appetite, and the body you live in
Attention and time
Time, attention, distraction, and where your days go

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Where your energy and your evenings go
  • one more episode past midnight
  • a morning with energy to spare
  • the rest you meant to take

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

Some mornings you wake with more than enough. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
energy since winter

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When you wake with new energy, which waiting thing pulls at you first?”

Next on the way
