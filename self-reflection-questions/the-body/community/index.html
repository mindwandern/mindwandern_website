Where the body meets community

The body
Health, energy, appetite, and the body you live in
Community
Neighbors, shared spaces, and the people you almost know

Together, they cover situations like:

  • The regulars you move alongside
  • a lane kept for you at the pool
  • a walking partner moving away
  • being expected somewhere each week

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

The Path
the lane by the wall
At the pool, someone has moved their bag to keep a spot by the wall for you, as they did last week. The warm water makes your legs feel steadier.

A question on the way

Who in your regular group noticed when you were away?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When did you first notice someone keeping a spot for you?”

Next on the way

Which morning did you first feel the regulars expected you back?

Further up this Path

If the regulars stopped saving you a place tomorrow, what would you believe about how they see you?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Which of the regulars would you miss most if they stopped saving you a lane?”

Next on the way

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Next on the way

Who would you most want to thank for saving you a place?

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

Sometimes the loop you shared becomes yours alone. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the last box
A moving truck waits at the end of the block. Your neighbor comes down the steps with the last box, at the same hour you two always met to walk the loop.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When you picture the morning loop without your neighbor, what is missing first?”

Next on the way

What stays with you most: the hour, the pace, or having someone beside you?

Further up this Path

If no one walks the loop with you again, will you still go out at that hour?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who on your block have you ever walked with?”

Next on the way

Of all the mornings you walked together, which one do you return to most often?

Further up this Path

Once the loop is yours alone, who on the block would you want to meet along it?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What did walking with a neighbor give you that had nothing to do with the walking?”

Next on the way

Which small morning habit would you most want to keep, even after the person who shared it is gone?

Further up this Path

Once the loop is quieter, who on the block might you start saying good morning to?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Mind Wandern is a self-reflection app that finds the question worth asking yourself.
