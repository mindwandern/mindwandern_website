Home, place, and where you belong
The wider world
News, your country, and the world beyond it

Together, they cover situations like:

  • When the news comes close to home
  • a council vote on your main street
  • a flood in a family member’s town
  • new apartments planned where the old shops are

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 1

Now find your question.

Follow one Path a few steps: take the fork that speaks to you more, and see the question it leads to.

Those are example Paths, shortened for this page.

The Path
the flyer about the bakery
The bakery on the corner is on the council agenda next week. A neighbor has left a folded flyer asking whether you will come and speak.

A question on the way

Which shop on your main street would you miss first?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What would it mean to you if the small shops on your street were still there in ten years?”

Next on the way

If the council votes to replace the shops on your street, what will you wish you had said?

Further up this Path

Which neighbors you already talk to would be glad to hear you speak at the meeting?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “If the bakery on the corner closed, who on your street would notice most?”

Next on the way

Which decision about your street have you already made, without a meeting or a vote?

Further up this Path

Which small place in your week would you keep, whatever happens to your street?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When you think of speaking at a council meeting, what makes you hesitate first?”

Next on the way

Which shop owner on your street would you least want to let down?

Further up this Path

After a council vote on your street, what will you do first to keep it feeling like yours?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

News from a family member’s town can fill a whole week. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
brown water in her street
Your sister’s street is under brown water in the photos on your phone. She has asked again whether you can come, and you keep scrolling past the same gray doorway.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “How does your sister’s message feel to you right now?”

Next on the way

Which ways to help your sister have you already weighed?

Further up this Path

What could you do from where you are that your sister would be glad to hear about?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What did you feel first when you saw the water in your sister’s town?”

Next on the way

When you scroll past the same photo again, what do you hope to find in it?

Further up this Path

What would you have to set aside this week to say yes to your sister?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Which part of your sister’s asking stays on your mind?”

Next on the way

When your sister asks again, what can you actually give her?

Further up this Path

In the coming weeks, what would going to your sister’s town change for you?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Mind Wandern is a self-reflection app that finds the question worth asking yourself.

Pick the topics on your mind. When the way forks, pick the prompt that speaks to you more, until you reach the one question worth asking. There is nothing to write, so it stays private.
