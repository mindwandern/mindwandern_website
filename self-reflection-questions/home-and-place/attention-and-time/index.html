After dinner, everyone drifts to a different room with a different screen. At your mother’s table, your phone lights up beside the teacup every few minutes. Home and place
Home, place, and where you belong
Attention and time
Time, attention, distraction, and where your days go

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Under one roof, on separate screens
  • evenings when everyone drifts to another room
  • a phone on the table during a visit home
  • a screen glowing after the house is asleep

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

A visit home is short, and the phone is there for all of it. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
tea at your mother’s table
Your mother pours tea in the house you grew up in. Your phone lies face-up beside the cup, lighting up every few minutes with messages from your life elsewhere.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When your phone lights up during a visit home, what pulls you toward it?”

Next on the way

Before you look, what do you want a message to give you that the people across from you cannot?

Further up this Path

Once a visit home is over, what will you remember of the hours you spent there?
