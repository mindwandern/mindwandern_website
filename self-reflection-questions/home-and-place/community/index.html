Home and place + Community

Self-reflection questions about neighbors, and the street that becomes home.

A bag of tomatoes over the fence from a neighbor whose name you just learned. A loaf saved for you at the corner store, the month you are moving away. A street becomes home through people you barely know. Start where Home and place meets Community, and pick the prompt that speaks to you more.

Where home meets community

Home and place
Home, place, and where you belong
Community
Neighbors, shared spaces, and the people you almost know

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Arriving on a street, or leaving one
  • the first real hello with a new neighbor
  • favors passed back and forth over the fence
  • telling the regulars you are moving away

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

Leaving a street takes more goodbyes than you expect. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the loaf he saves for you
The man at the corner store has started saving your usual loaf without being asked. You have not told him you are moving away.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “Which part of telling the man at the corner store you are leaving is hardest?”

Next on the way

Of the people who see you each week, whose face would you miss most?

Further up this Path

Which stranger who knew your order will you think of first, years from now?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would it mean to you if the shopkeeper you see each week never learned you were leaving?”

Next on the way

Which of your small goodbyes before a move has felt most like home?

Further up this Path

Which person you know only by sight would you be sorry to lose from your days?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who else in town knows your usual order?”

Next on the way

Who on your street has started treating you as someone who is leaving?

Further up this Path

Which small weekly routine on your street will you keep, long after you move?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.
