Home and place + The body

Self-reflection questions about rest, aging in place and the home you live in.

A bus starts outside just as you fall asleep after a night shift. Your hand stops on the banister at the third step. A home is where your body rests, or tries to. Start where Home and place meets The body, and pick the prompt that speaks to you more.

Where home meets the body

Home and place
Home, place, and where you belong
The body
Health, energy, appetite, and the body you live in

Together, they cover situations like:

  • How your home fits your body
  • sleeping by day after a night shift
  • stairs that ask more of you each year
  • an afternoon nap you cannot let yourself take

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

A house can feel different on the stairs, a little more each year. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the third step
Your hand finds the banister on the third step, as it always does. This time you stop there for a moment before going on.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “Which step in your house has started to slow you down?”

Next on the way

When you stop on the stairs now, what does your body ask of you first?

Further up this Path

What do you want the people who love you to understand about staying in your home?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When did the stairs in your house first feel different under your hand?”

Next on the way

When someone sees you pause on the stairs, what do you hope they will not say?

Further up this Path

What in your home do you most want to keep as it is in the years ahead?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would you lose first if your house stopped fitting your habits?”

Next on the way

If the stairs meant you had to leave your house, what would you be leaving behind?

Further up this Path

Which of your daily routines would you miss most if you could no longer do it?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.
