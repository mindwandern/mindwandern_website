Community + Self and change

Self-reflection questions about volunteering, being known, and who you are becoming around others.

The food bank sign-up sheet, and your name not on it this time. A woman at your Saturday class who says your name warmly, when you never learned hers. Who you are among people you half know can change quietly. Start where Community meets Self and change, and pick the prompt that speaks to you more.

Where community meets change

Community
Neighbors, shared spaces, and the people you almost know
Self and change
Identity, change, starting out or starting over, and who you are becoming

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Who you become among familiar faces
  • a volunteer shift you might give up
  • the name you never asked for
  • letting people you half know see more of you

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 1

Now find your question.

Follow one Path a few steps: take the fork that speaks to you more, and see the question it leads to.

Those are example Paths, shortened for this page.

The Path
the sign-up sheet
You have volunteered at the food bank every Thursday for months. The sign-up sheet is in front of you, and this time your name is not on it yet.

A question on the way

If you gave up a weekly commitment, where would you actually feel the gap?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What is keeping your name off the sign-up sheet this time?”

Next on the way

Across all your volunteer shifts, which moment with someone has stayed with you longest?

Further up this Path

Which part of who you are when you volunteer do you want to keep, whether or not your name is on the list?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who would notice if your name stayed off the volunteer sheet?”

Next on the way

If you stopped volunteering for good, what about yourself would change?

Further up this Path

Whose kindness on a volunteer shift would you want to thank, if you saw them again?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What has volunteering given you that you have not found somewhere else?”

Next on the way

When the work goes quiet and you are just beside someone, what are your hands doing?

Further up this Path

Of the people you have worked beside, which one would you most want to tell that you are leaving?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

A name can go unasked for months. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the name you never asked
At your Saturday class, a woman you have nodded to for months says your name warmly. You never learned hers, and you are still deciding what to say back.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When someone says your name warmly, what do you feel in the moment before you answer?”

Next on the way

When someone greets you warmly, how much of yourself do you still hold back?

Further up this Path

Which small habit of yours keeps people you half know at a comfortable distance?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What is hardest to admit about not knowing someone’s name?”

Next on the way

What would asking someone’s name now show about the months you let pass?

Further up this Path

Which small moment this past month made you wish you had asked someone’s name sooner?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “After months of nodding, what would asking for a name now change?”

Next on the way

Which people have you talked with for months without asking them one question about their own life?

Further up this Path

When someone says your name first, what do you feel pulled to give back about yourself?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.
