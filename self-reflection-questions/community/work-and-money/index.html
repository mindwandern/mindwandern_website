Work and money
Work, money, and what you earn, keep, or lose

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Money among neighbors and regulars
  • splitting the cost of something everyone uses
  • a market stall and the faces that come back each week
  • chipping in beside people you know only by first name

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

The Path
the shared water line
You stand with neighbors you know only by first name. Fixing the shared water line will cost more than anyone expected, and someone has to say who pays.

A question on the way

When did you last chip in for something everyone around you uses?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “Whose face comes to mind first when you picture neighbors meeting about money?”

Next on the way

When a price is named and the room goes quiet, what does the quiet ask of you?

Further up this Path

Where in your life are you bound to people by a shared cost, not by anything you chose?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Where in your days has a shared cost fallen to you?”

Next on the way

When a shared cost needs settling, whose hand goes up first?

Further up this Path

If you alone paid for something shared, and no one ever stepped in, would you keep showing up?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What happens for you when you stand in a circle of people you only half know?”

Next on the way

When a bill is split among neighbors, whose share would be hardest for you to ask for?

Further up this Path

Whose shared costs have you covered without anyone asking you to?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

A stall can feel like yours long before it pays. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the stall fee
The fee for your Saturday market stall has gone up again. You count the coins in the tin while the regulars ask about next week.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When a fee rises, which regular do you think of first?”

Next on the way

What would your regulars say they lost if you were gone?

Further up this Path

When money gets tight, which of the people you see each week would you ask for help first?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What has your stall cost you this month, beyond the money?”

Next on the way

When the fee rises and sales dip, what do you tell yourself about staying?

Further up this Path

Whatever happens to the fee, what do you want to keep about the work you keep showing up for?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When did the market square first start to feel like yours?”

Next on the way

What has a regular given you over the years that never shows up in your sales?

Further up this Path

If someone else paid the fee, would you still feel the square needs you there?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.
