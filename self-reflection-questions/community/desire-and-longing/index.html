Where community meets longing

Community
Neighbors, shared spaces, and the people you almost know
Desire and longing
Desire, longing, love, play, and what you want but cannot name

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Wanting to belong among near-strangers
  • the guests who stay to help clear up
  • the same faces on a park bench
  • hoping an acquaintance becomes a friend

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

The Path
the last two guests
The party is over, but two neighbors are still drying glasses at your sink, slowly, not yet ready to leave the evening behind.

A question on the way

After the last guest has gone, what do you want the evening to have given you?

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

Easy company can be the part of the day you look forward to most. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the warm end of the bench
The park bench is half-taken when you arrive. Someone has saved you the warm end, and talk of ducks and the late frost drifts between you and people you have never called by name.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When you sit down with familiar faces, what do you notice first in yourself?”

Next on the way

How much of what you want from your days do light, repeated meetings give you?

Further up this Path

Where else in your week are there people who expect nothing from you but your presence?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What do you find you need from small talk about the weather?”

Next on the way

When a stranger is warm to you, what do you do with that warmth?

Further up this Path

Which person you sit with each day would you most want to still be there a year from now?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When did you last feel that a place was saved for you?”

Next on the way

If the familiar faces stopped coming tomorrow, what would you have been counting on without saying so?

Further up this Path

Who in your week would you tell first if some small news about you changed?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.
