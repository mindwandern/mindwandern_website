How it works Topics

Self-reflection questions about the wider world, and finding the one question worth asking yourself.

The wider world is too much ground for one good question: the headlines before sleep, an election, prices at the checkout, a vote about your street, a flood where family lives. So a list of questions about all of it stays general. Add a second topic, and combined they get you closer to the question you were looking for, even if you did not know it yet.

Together, they hold situations like:

  • When the news follows you to bed
  • scrolling the headlines before sleep
  • an alert that arrives during family time
  • choosing what you let in

In Mind Wandern, a Path is a short series of self-reflection questions that starts somewhere in that combined ground.

In the app you can add more topics, or keep one away. For example:

  • + People close to you → find situations where the news reaches you through family far away
  • − People close to you → keep family away, and focus the situations on you and the screen

  • Why not just a list of questions about the world?

    A list might ask

    How do you stay informed without feeling overwhelmed? What change do you want to see in the world?

    Lists like that are written for anyone, about any headline. They are a fine start, and if a list and a notebook are all you need, that is a good way to reflect.

    But when the news is on your mind, it usually lands somewhere close: your paycheck, a parent, your street, a tired body. Starting there gets you to a question you can actually answer.
