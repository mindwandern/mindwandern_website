Together, they cover situations like:

  • Policy that lands on your paycheck
  • delivering hard news you did not decide
  • a new tax and your first pay stub
  • a contract that depends on next year’s budget

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

A first paycheck raises questions nobody warned you about. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the first pay stub
Three weeks into your first job on a construction site, your coworkers say a new housing tax will change everything. You check your pay stub twice before lunch.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

Next on the way
