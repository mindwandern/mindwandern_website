Where the world meets community

The wider world
News, your country, and the town you live in
Community
Neighbors, shared spaces, and the people you almost know

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Local losses and new arrivals
  • a hospital ward in town that closed
  • a family new to the building
  • talk with strangers in a waiting room

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Now find your question.

Follow one Path a few steps: take the fork that speaks to you more, and see the question it leads to.

Those are example Paths, shortened for this page.

The Path
the ward that closed
In a waiting room, the woman beside you says the hospital ward in town closed last spring. Her mother now sleeps forty minutes away, so the bus ride is part of every visit.

A question on the way

When a person you barely know shares a loss with you, what are they asking you to carry?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When have you sat beside a stranger who spoke about a loss in your town?”

Next on the way

Whose private worry did you first hear on a bus or in a waiting room?

Further up this Path

When a local service closed, what did you lose that no one named?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Which closed service in your town have you noticed missing?”

Next on the way

Whose need for help have you been carrying alongside your own, without anyone asking you to?

Further up this Path

Which small kindness from a stranger in a waiting room would you most want to offer back?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who in your life has had to travel far for care they once reached on foot?”

Next on the way

What will you choose to say out loud about a loss your town is quietly carrying?

Further up this Path

Which person beside you on the bus or in a waiting room will you ask how their visit went?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

Sometimes the news moves in upstairs. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
a meal for the new family
The family on the third floor moved in last month with one suitcase. The neighborhood group is asking who can bring a warm meal before the week ends.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “Who around you would you most want to know is being fed?”

Next on the way

Where does the wish to help end, and the wish to be sure you are helping rightly begin?

Further up this Path

Once the meal is dropped off, what will you still want to ask the people on your street?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What makes a meal for strangers feel like something you could give?”

Next on the way

Who on your street or in your building has ever brought you something warm?

Further up this Path

Which person you already know would you ask to come with you when you knock?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When did you last carry something to a door you had never knocked on before?”

Next on the way

When you picture knocking with food in your hands, what do you fear will happen next?

Further up this Path

If you left the food at the door and no one answered, what would you still feel you had given?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.
