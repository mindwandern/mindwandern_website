Where longing meets the people close to you

People close to you
Family, friends, coworkers, and others in your life
Desire and longing
Desire, longing, love, play, and what you want but cannot name

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Wanting more of someone’s company
  • a message you keep rewriting
  • a friendship that lives in one shared ritual
  • saying plainly what you want from someone

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 1

Now find your question.

Follow one Path a few steps: take the fork that speaks to you more, and see the question it leads to.

Those are example Paths, shortened for this page.

The Path
the message rewritten four times
Your thumb hovers over a message you have rewritten four times, to someone whose morning is just starting on the other side of the world.

A question on the way

Whose voice would you most like to hear when your day begins?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What feels hardest about sending it: the wording, or the waiting?”

Next on the way

What do you picture happening in the hours after you press send?

Further up this Path

If you told someone plainly that you miss them, what are you afraid it would show?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When did you first start rewriting it?”

Next on the way

What stopped you from sending the first version?

Further up this Path

What do you edit out when you write to someone you miss?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would sending it unpolished show about what you want?”

Next on the way

What do you want them to feel first when they read it?

Further up this Path

What is the one thing you want someone far away to know you feel?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

Missing someone does not settle in one evening. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the same four bars
Once a month you and your oldest friend play music together, still stumbling over the same four bars, and you both call it the best hour of the month.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What do you look forward to most in the hour with your old friend?”

Next on the way

What about being together would you not trade for better music?

Further up this Path

Which of your friend’s small habits do you count on without ever saying so?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would you miss most if your monthly hour together stopped?”

Next on the way

What do you keep doing only so you can sit beside someone you love?

Further up this Path

What would you want your friend to keep doing, even in months when you have nothing to say?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What does your friend bring to the room that no one else could?”

Next on the way

When you both stall on the same bars and laugh, what do you want from that friend?

Further up this Path

Whose company do you wait for all month?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Mind Wandern is a self-reflection app that finds the question worth asking yourself.

Pick the topics on your mind. When the way forks, pick the prompt that speaks to you more, until you reach the one question worth asking. There is nothing to write, so it stays private.
