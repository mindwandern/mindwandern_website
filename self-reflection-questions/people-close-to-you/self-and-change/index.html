Where change meets the people close to you

People close to you
Family, friends, coworkers, and others in your life
Self and change
Identity, change, starting out or starting over, and who you are becoming

Together, they cover situations like:

  • When someone close changes, or you do
  • a grown child moving far away
  • being the one who keeps the peace
  • a new friend who only knows who you are now

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 1

Now find your question.

Follow one Path a few steps: take the fork that speaks to you more, and see the question it leads to.

Those are example Paths, shortened for this page.

The Path
the news over the kettle
Your daughter has just told you she is moving across the country for a job you did not know she wanted. You are still holding the kettle.

A question on the way

Who in your life has changed the most while you were not looking?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When your daughter told you she was moving away, what did you feel first?”

Next on the way

Before she said it, what did you already know about her plans?

Further up this Path

How do you let a grown child become someone you do not know yet?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What weighs on you more: the distance, or not knowing her plans?”

Next on the way

What do you hope your daughter will tell you herself, without being asked?

Further up this Path

If your grown child asked about your life right now, what would you want to be able to answer?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What did you once imagine her life would look like?”

Next on the way

What do you wonder about the person your daughter is becoming?

Further up this Path

If her life moves somewhere you cannot follow, what part of your own days would you let change?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

A role in a family can set over years. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the bread between them
Your brother and your father have not spoken since an argument at Easter. At dinner, you are the one who passes the bread between them.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When two people in your family stop speaking, what keeps you in the middle?”

Next on the way

What have you set aside at that table that you no longer expect anyone to notice?

Further up this Path

Of the years you have spent in the middle, which still feels like your own choice?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who at the table expects you to keep the peace without being asked?”

Next on the way

When did you first notice dinner would go quiet unless you spoke?

Further up this Path

Who would you be at the family table if you stopped keeping the peace?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would happen if you stayed quiet and let them talk to each other?”

Next on the way

What have you stopped saying to either of them?

Further up this Path

What have you quietly set aside so the people around you could keep their footing?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Mind Wandern is a self-reflection app that finds the question worth asking yourself.

Pick the topics on your mind. When the way forks, pick the prompt that speaks to you more, until you reach the one question worth asking. There is nothing to write, so it stays private.
