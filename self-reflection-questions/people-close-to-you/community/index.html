Where community meets the people close to you

People close to you
Family, friends, coworkers, and others in your life
Community
Neighbors, shared spaces, and the people you almost know

Together, they cover situations like:

  • When the circle gets smaller
  • a weekly supper after friends move away
  • the group chat asking who is coming
  • visiting a parent in care, and what you tell the family

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 1

Now find your question.

Follow one Path a few steps: take the fork that speaks to you more, and see the question it leads to.

Those are example Paths, shortened for this page.

The Path
the weekly supper
Two of the group have moved away, and the pot is too big now. The group chat asks whether you will cook again on Thursday.

A question on the way

Which evening with your friends from the past year do you remember most clearly?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “Which of the friends who moved away do you still think about on Thursdays?”

Next on the way

Whose empty seat is hardest to picture filled again?

Further up this Path

Which gathering would you keep going even if nobody asked you to?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “If the weekly supper quietly stopped this month, what would change for you?”

Next on the way

Which part of the evening would you miss most?

Further up this Path

What would you need to hear from the others to feel asked, not told?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who in the group would you most hate to disappoint by saying no?”

Next on the way

When you picture saying yes, what do you feel: relief, or the weight of who is missing?

Further up this Path

Which answer to your friends would you most regret?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

Caring for a parent goes on for months. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
how your mother seemed today
You visit your mother at the care home each afternoon. Then your sister’s voice note asks how she seemed today, and you decide what to tell her.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What do you want your sister to hear in your answer?”

Next on the way

Of all the things you could tell her, which one would you most want to be true?

Further up this Path

What have you let your family believe, to spare them, that is not quite true?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When family asks how your mother is, which part is hardest to tell?”

Next on the way

When the staff greet you by your mother’s name, what does that leave with you?

Further up this Path

What does the rest of your family not know about the hours you spend caring?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Since the visits became yours, what has gone quiet between you and your sister?”

Next on the way

What do you want your sister to understand about the hours you spend visiting?

Further up this Path

What would change between you and your sister if she knew how your mother really is?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Mind Wandern is a self-reflection app that finds the question worth asking yourself.

Pick the topics on your mind. When the way forks, pick the prompt that speaks to you more, until you reach the one question worth asking. There is nothing to write, so it stays private.
