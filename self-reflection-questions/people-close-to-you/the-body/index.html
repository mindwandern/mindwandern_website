People close to you + The body

Self-reflection questions about tiredness, hunger and the people you look after.

The cook who eats last, the parent awake at three, the one who notices everyone’s tiredness but their own. Start where People close to you meets The body, and pick the prompt that speaks to you more.

Where the body meets the people close to you

People close to you
Family, friends, coworkers, and others in your life
The body
Health, energy, appetite, and the body you live in

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Caring for others before yourself
  • cooking for everyone and eating last
  • night feeds, and who sleeps first
  • tiredness nobody at home sees

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 1

Now find your question.

Follow one Path a few steps: take the fork that speaks to you more, and see the question it leads to.

Those are example Paths, shortened for this page.

The Path
the cook who eats last
The pot is on the table and everyone is reaching for seconds. You are still at the stove, and no one has asked what you would like.

A question on the way

When during your day do you notice your own hunger?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When did you last sit down to a meal you did not cook for others?”

Next on the way

When did you last wait to be fed something you had not made yourself?

Further up this Path

Which of your own needs have you put off the longest?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who in your household has ever asked what you would like to eat?”

Next on the way

When someone did ask, what did you say?

Further up this Path

What would it feel like to be the one someone thinks to ask?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would you choose for yourself tonight, if no one else were hungry?”

Next on the way

When did you last put your own appetite first, even for a moment?

Further up this Path

Who at your table has ever wondered what you are hungry for?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

Nights like this last for months, not one night. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
who sleeps first
It is three in the morning. Your partner stands in the doorway with the baby’s bottle, and your tea has gone cold twice tonight.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “Which of you will be awake longest tonight?”

Next on the way

When the tea goes cold again, what do you quietly take on so your partner can rest?

Further up this Path

When you are both too tired to talk, what do you most need your partner to understand?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When you hand over the baby, what do you wish your partner knew?”

Next on the way

How do you tell your partner you are running out of steam?

Further up this Path

After a long night, what would let you believe you did enough?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who would take over so you could sleep first tonight?”

Next on the way

What do you need from your partner that you never say out loud?

Further up this Path

What have you stopped asking for without anyone noticing?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.
