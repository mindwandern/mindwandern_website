People close to you + The wider world

Self-reflection questions about family far away, and the world between you.

A letter about the price of bread, a flight booked for spring, a parent’s rent going up: the news often reaches you through the people you love. Start where People close to you meets The wider world, and pick the prompt that speaks to you more.

Where the wider world meets the people close to you

People close to you
Family, friends, coworkers, and others in your life
The wider world
News, your country, and the town you live in

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Family across borders, and rising prices
  • sending money home
  • a sibling moving abroad while a parent stays
  • news you first hear from family

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

The Path
a message about the price of bread
Your sister writes from home that bread costs more every week. You read the message twice, wondering how far the money you send will go.

A question on the way

Which person you love can you reach only by message or call?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “When family writes about money troubles back home, what catches your attention first?”

Next on the way

Which line in a letter from home do you reread: the prices, or how they are?

Further up this Path

Beyond the money you send, who do you want to be to family far away?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who in your life has asked you to help with a cost from far away?”

Next on the way

What did the last request ask of you that you had not planned for?

Further up this Path

Of the money you send abroad, which part did you choose?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “From far away, what can you actually do for family who are struggling?”

Next on the way

When you think about what you can really send, where is the honest limit?

Further up this Path

When you send money across a distance, what does the amount leave out?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

A move abroad is planned for months. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the flight booked for spring
Your sister is moving abroad in the spring. Your mother is staying, and her rent is about to go up.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What did you feel first when your sister booked her flight abroad?”

Next on the way

What have you not yet said to your sister about her leaving?

Further up this Path

When a sibling moves abroad, what can you still promise the parent who stays?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When your mother brings up the rent, what is she not saying?”

Next on the way

Whose help do you wish your mother would accept?

Further up this Path

Which promise to your mother can you not keep yet?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When you picture the spring, which moment do you dread most?”

Next on the way

Once your sister has left, what falls to you?

Further up this Path

When a sibling moves away, which small weekly ritual with a parent do you want to keep?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Mind Wandern is a self-reflection app that finds the question worth asking yourself.

Pick the topics on your mind. When the way forks, pick the prompt that speaks to you more, until you reach the one question worth asking. There is nothing to write, so it stays private.
