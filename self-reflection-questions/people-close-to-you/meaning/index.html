Where meaning meets the people close to you

People close to you
Family, friends, coworkers, and others in your life
Meaning
Purpose, faith, and the questions you have no answers to

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Faith, hope and the people you love
  • a grown child asking what you believe
  • a family member who stopped coming
  • what you hope to pass on

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 1

Now find your question.

Follow one Path a few steps: take the fork that speaks to you more, and see the question it leads to.

Those are example Paths, shortened for this page.

The Path
the smaller question
Over cold coffee, your grown son asks whether you still pray. It is a smaller question than the one he meant.

A question on the way

Where in your days do you show what you believe, without having to say it?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What was your son really asking?”

Next on the way

If your answer is not yes or no, what would you want it to be?

Further up this Path

What do you hope your son will still carry from you when he is your age?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What made the quiet after your son’s question feel so heavy?”

Next on the way

What did you want to say in that silence?

Further up this Path

When you think about what your life is for, what do you still hold on to?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would you most want your son to understand about where you stand now?”

Next on the way

What do you find hardest to say to him?

Further up this Path

What is one plain thing you want your son to hear from you?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

Some hopes last for years. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the extra plate
Every Sunday you set out one plate more than the number who will sit down, and wonder whether your brother will text that he is nearly there.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What do you picture your brother making of the extra plate?”

Next on the way

Across from the empty place, which comes back first: the old argument or the old laugh?

Further up this Path

If he walked in tomorrow, what would you need him to have given up?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who else at the table notices the empty chair?”

Next on the way

When your brother’s name comes up, who goes quiet first?

Further up this Path

If your brother never came again, what would setting his place still mean to you?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When did you last really expect your brother to come?”

Next on the way

When he comes, what do you want to say that you have not said before?

Further up this Path

What do you do with the hope that someone will come back?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Mind Wandern is a self-reflection app that finds the question worth asking yourself.

Pick the topics on your mind. When the way forks, pick the prompt that speaks to you more, until you reach the one question worth asking. There is nothing to write, so it stays private.
