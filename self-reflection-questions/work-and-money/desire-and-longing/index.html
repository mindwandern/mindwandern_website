Where work meets what you still want

Work and money
Work, money, and what you earn, keep, or lose
Desire and longing
Desire, longing, love, play, and what you want but cannot name

Together, they cover situations like:

  • The work you want, beside the job you have
  • making things no one pays you for
  • a dream on hold for the pay
  • wanting more, or wanting enough

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 1

Now find your question.

Follow one Path a few steps: take the fork that speaks to you more, and see the question it leads to.

The Path
the workbench on Saturday
On Saturday the workbench is yours, and the wood you cut yesterday is waiting to become what you have pictured all week.

A question on the way

What do you make or do that no one pays you for?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What does making give you that the working week never does?”

Next on the way

When did you first feel that?

Further up this Path

What would change if your job gave you a little of that too?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would change if someone offered to pay you for it?”

Next on the way

What would you be afraid of losing?

Further up this Path

What about the making would you never want anyone to put a price on?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When a piece goes wrong, how do you feel about it?”

Next on the way

How is that different from a mistake at your paid work?

Further up this Path

What does that difference tell you about the work you would want to do?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the job that was never the dream
The same salary arrives each month, and a small wish to make something useless and joyful waits at the edge of your desk.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “Which part of your working week feels like the price of your salary?”

Next on the way

What do you lose in those hours that you would want to keep?

Further up this Path

What would you give up from your paid hours to make time for the wish?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What has the steady pay bought you, besides the bills?”

Next on the way

Once the bills are paid, what do you still want to spend yourself on?

Further up this Path

If the bills were already paid, what would you make with your own hands?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “When does the small wish come back to you most often?”

Next on the way

What do you do with it when it does?

Further up this Path

If the wish never became anything useful, would you still want to keep it?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.
