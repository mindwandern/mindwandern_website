Self-reflection questions about work, and finding the one question worth asking yourself.

Work and money is too much ground for one good question, so a list of questions about all of it stays general. Add a second topic, and combined they get you closer to the question you were looking for, even if you did not know it yet.

Home, place, and where you belong

Together, they hold situations like:

  • Moving for a job
  • a job in another town
  • working from home
  • a long commute

In Mind Wandern, a Path is a short series of self-reflection questions that starts somewhere in that combined ground.

In the app you can add more topics, or keep one away. For example:

  • + The past → focus the situations on work that ended
  • − The past → keep the past away, and focus the situations on what comes next

  • Why not just a list of questions about work?

    A list might ask

    What do you value at work? Where do you see yourself in five years?

    Lists like that are written for anyone. They are a fine start, and if a list and a notebook are all you need, that is a good way to reflect.

    But when something about work is on your mind, it is usually tied to something else: the people at home, the hours, what it all means to you. Starting there gets you to a question you can actually answer.

The whole Path, with every topic, in the app

Mind Wandern is a self-reflection app that finds the question worth asking yourself.

Pick the topics on your mind. When the way forks, pick the prompt that speaks to you more, until you reach the one question worth asking. There is nothing to write, so it stays private.
