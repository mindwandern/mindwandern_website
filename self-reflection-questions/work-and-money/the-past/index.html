Where work meets what has ended

Work and money
Work, money, and what you earn, keep, or lose
The past
Memory, loss, and the time before now

Together, they cover situations like:

  • After a job ends
  • the routines a job leaves behind
  • the people you no longer see every day
  • a job you still think about

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

An ending can stay with you long after the last day. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the badge you hand back
You are back at your old workplace to hand in your badge. Former colleagues walk past while you wait at the door.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What did you expect to feel, standing at that door?”

Next on the way

What did you feel instead?

Further up this Path

What would it take to stop waiting to be asked back?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Which of the people walking past still knows your name?”

Next on the way

Who would you want to stop and talk?

Further up this Path

Who from that job do you want to keep in your life, now that work no longer brings you together?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would you say if someone asked how you have been?”

Next on the way

What would the true answer be?

Further up this Path

Since you left, what has become of the person who walked through that door each morning?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Mind Wandern is a self-reflection app that finds the question worth asking yourself.

Pick the topics on your mind. When the way forks, pick the prompt that speaks to you more, until you reach the one question worth asking. There is nothing to write, so it stays private.
