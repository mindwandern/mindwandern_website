Where work meets meaning

Work and money
Work, money, and what you earn, keep, or lose
Meaning
Purpose, faith, and the questions you have no answers to

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Why you keep doing the work
  • work you would do unpaid
  • what the pay cannot tell you
  • work and what you believe in

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

Walk 1

Now find your question.

Follow one Path a few steps: take the fork that speaks to you more, and see the question it leads to.

Those are example Paths, shortened for this page.

The Path
the end of the night shift
Your night shift on the ward ends at dawn. The patient who asked for you by name is asleep, and you wonder whether this is still the work you chose.

A question on the way

What first drew you to the work you do?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What do you feel most about the care you gave tonight?”

Next on the way

Which of the hours you gave this year would you give again?

Further up this Path

What do you need to be true about why you keep coming back?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What did the work promise you when you started?”

Next on the way

Which of those promises has it kept on the hardest nights?

Further up this Path

If the work never got easier, what would still make it yours?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What does your pay tell you about why you stay?”

Next on the way

Which part of the work would you still do if the pay stopped?

Further up this Path

What do the hours you give say about what matters to you?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

A question about your work can stay on your mind for months. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the shifts you sign up for
You sign up for the food bank rota again tonight. Your belief in feeding people has not changed, but the rent keeps rising.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What does feeding people mean to you?”

Next on the way

When did you last feel sure a shift mattered?

Further up this Path

If the rent kept rising and your belief never wavered, which would you let go of first?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What does the rent weigh against your hours there?”

Next on the way

What part of the money worry comes with you into the shift?

Further up this Path

How much can you give before the giving starts to cost the people at home?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who do you picture on the other side of the counter?”

Next on the way

Whose face comes to mind first when your shift begins?

Further up this Path

What would it mean to you if no one there ever knew your name?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Mind Wandern is a self-reflection app that finds the question worth asking yourself.

Pick the topics on your mind. When the way forks, pick the prompt that speaks to you more, until you reach the one question worth asking. There is nothing to write, so it stays private.
