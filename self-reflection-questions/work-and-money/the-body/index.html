Work and money + The body

Self-reflection questions about what work asks of your body.

Shoulders that rise with the inbox, legs that ache after a shift, a lunch you forgot to eat: your body keeps its own account of work. Start where Work and money meets The body, and pick the prompt that speaks to you more.

Where work meets the body

Work and money
Work, money, and what you earn, keep, or lose
The body
Health, energy, appetite, and the body you live in

Together, they cover situations like:

  • When work wears on your body
  • stress you carry in your shoulders
  • the tiredness after a long shift
  • lunch at your desk, or no lunch at all

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.
