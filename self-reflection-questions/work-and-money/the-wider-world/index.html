Where work meets the wider world

Work and money
Work, money, and what you earn, keep, or lose
The wider world
News, your country, and the town you live in

Together, they cover situations like:

  • Work in uncertain times
  • job cuts you first hear about on the news
  • a council vote on your workplace
  • prices rising faster than pay

Mind Wandern starts a Path somewhere in that combined ground, and your picks along the way narrow it down. In the app you can set it more precisely still: place offerings on more topics, or a ward to keep a topic away.

The Path
the vote on the depot
On Thursday the council votes on closing the bus depot where you work. You have two minutes at the microphone.

A question on the way

If you had two minutes to say what your work is worth, what would you say?

Then the way forks. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “Which part of speaking up feels heaviest?”

Next on the way

What are you afraid will happen if your voice shakes?

Further up this Path

Whatever the room decides, what do you want to be proud of having said?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who are you speaking for, besides yourself?”

Next on the way

Which of them would you most want to hear you?

Further up this Path

What does the depot give the town that a vote cannot count?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would change in your days if the depot closed?”

Next on the way

Which part of your mornings would you miss first?

Further up this Path

If the depot closes, what will you tell yourself about the years you gave it?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

Walk 2 · another day

When something stays unresolved, each walk takes a new way up.

Uncertain times rarely settle in a week. On another day, the same two topics start a different Path, and it reaches a different question.

The Path
the cuts on the radio
The radio says your employer is cutting night crews. Nobody has said whose jobs are on the list.

The way forks right away. Tap the prompt that speaks to you more.

Pick a prompt above to see where it leads. Each one reaches a different question.

If you pick “What did you do in the hour after you heard the news?”

Next on the way

Who did you want to talk to first?

Further up this Path

Whatever happens, what about these years of work do you want to keep?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “Who would feel the cut before you did?”

Next on the way

What would you tell them tonight, before anything is certain?

Further up this Path

What do the people who depend on your pay need from you now, besides the pay?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.

If you pick “What would you still call your work if this job ended?”

Next on the way

Which of your skills do not belong to this employer?

Further up this Path

If you had to start again, what would you want the next job to have that this one did not?

Answer it in your head, or on paper if you like.
