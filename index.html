Why we built privacy the hard way.

Self-reflection gets personal quickly.

Nothing to leak, and nothing to sell. Mind Wandern holds no names, emails or passwords. So if our servers were ever hacked, there would be nothing to leak. An account not visited for over 90 days is deleted with everything in it, and data that is deleted can’t be sold later, even if someone buys the company one day.

Your answers are never stored. A journaling app has to keep what you write, on its own servers or in someone’s cloud. Mind Wandern has no text box, so there is nothing of yours to keep. Your answers stay with you, or in a paper notebook.

No ad code in the app. Ad software inside an app can report what you do there to an ad network. Mind Wandern has none, so no ad network can watch what you do or run its code while you walk a Path.

We still buy ads outside the app, so people can find Mind Wandern, but those ads work less well. We can’t follow anyone from an ad into the app, or ask an ad network to find people like you. If you think this is the right choice, telling a friend helps us more than any ad we can buy.

Usage counts are for fixing the app. The app sends a few anonymous counts, such as “a Path was finished”, and crash reports. We use them to find what’s broken, never to make you spend more time in the app. Both have their own switch in Settings › Privacy.

We delete the launch list. Companies often keep a mailing list for years, so they can keep writing to you. We delete ours after the second letter, or by the end of 2027 at the latest.

If you opt into email launch reminders, the list holds only your email address. Buttondown sends the two letters for us. Buttondown and the services it relies on are bound to use your address only for those letters, until the list is deleted. The list is kept apart from the app, so your address is never linked to what you do there.

Questions

Who makes Mind Wandern, and why?

One developer, Tony Targonski. I started Mind Wandern after a corporate layoff. A lot felt unresolved all at once: untangling a long career from who I am as a person, the coworkers suddenly gone, what gives things meaning now. I could name most of the themes. What I needed was help narrowing them down to specific questions I could work through. I built Mind Wandern to do that for me, and I think it can do it for others too.

How is Mind Wandern different from journaling?

Journaling apps help you think by writing, but whatever you type is stored somewhere, by someone. Mind Wandern is an alternative to journaling apps, for people who want to reflect without writing. It has no text box, so you think things through in your head. If writing helps you, a paper notebook works well alongside the app. The writing stays in your drawer, not on someone’s server.

What makes Mind Wandern private?

Mind Wandern has nowhere to type, so your answers never leave your head. There is no sign-up, so the app holds no name, email or password. It has no ad code, no advertising ID and no tracking across apps. An account not visited for over 90 days is deleted, with everything in it.

The privacy policy lists everything the app keeps.

Does Mind Wandern use AI?

Yes. In Mind Wandern, a language model writes each Path, a short series of self-reflection questions, as you go. The illustrations are made with an image model. Requests to the model come from our servers, never from your phone, and carry no account ID, so nothing in them says whose Path it is. Each request goes only to model providers that have agreed to keep no copy of it (zero data retention) and not to use it for training. The model sees only text the app wrote: the topic, the questions so far, which way the Path went at each fork, and a few notes from recent Paths. It never sees what you think, because there is nowhere to write it down.

How is Mind Wandern different from a list of self-reflection questions?

A list of self-reflection questions gives everyone the same questions, and leaves you to pick where to start. A free list and a notebook are a good way to begin, and for many people that is enough. Mind Wandern narrows things down instead. It suggests a few specific places to start, such as “The evening you planned with someone”, drawn from the topics you asked to see more of. At each fork you pick the prompt that speaks to you more, and the next question follows from it, until the Path reaches the one self-reflection question worth asking yourself.

How is Mind Wandern different from meditation?

Meditation apps help calm a busy mind, usually by letting thoughts pass. Mind Wandern is an alternative to meditation apps, for when you would rather follow a thought than let it go. A series of questions leads you from one thought to the next. The two also fit together: meditation can quiet your mind before you begin, and a thought that keeps returning while you meditate can be the one you follow afterwards.

How is Mind Wandern different from therapy?

Mind Wandern is not therapy. It gives no advice and no diagnosis, and no therapist or coach is part of it. The AI in it only writes questions: there is no chat, nothing replies to what you think, and it keeps no profile of you. That makes it a private alternative to confiding in an AI chatbot, for when you want to think something through and keep your answers to yourself. You can use it alongside therapy, but it is not a replacement for therapy or any professional help. If you are struggling, please reach out to a doctor, a therapist or a local crisis line.

Does Mind Wandern track habits or send reminders?

No. Mind Wandern has no streaks, scores, badges or notifications, and the app never shows a count of how often you come or how long you stay. You open it when something is on your mind, not because the app asked. Nothing marks the days you stay away, and you can stop any Path partway. We never design the app to bring you back more often, or to keep you in it longer.

How long does a session of Mind Wandern take?

Five to fifteen minutes. In Mind Wandern, a session is called a Path: a short series of self-reflection questions. Each day offers a few Paths to choose from. A Path builds to one harder question, then slows down and comes to an end on its own, so you don’t have to decide when you’re done.

How much does Mind Wandern cost?

Your first Path in Mind Wandern is free. After that, new Paths need the Annual Pass, a yearly subscription through the App Store or Google Play. If you haven’t had the trial before, it starts with 14 days free, with your payment details on file. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you aren’t charged. Otherwise, the store charges the yearly price it shows you before you start, and renews it every year until you cancel in the store’s subscription settings. Apple and Google handle billing and refunds. Without the pass, you keep everything from the Paths you’ve already walked.

Why does Mind Wandern look like a comic?

Because I read them. After I was laid off, I finally had time again for hobbies I’d set aside for my career, and collecting and reading graphic novels was one I came back to. I was building Mind Wandern for myself first, so I wanted it to look like the things I love. The format also let me bring the “wandern” hike to a phone, for the days I couldn’t get out of the city. Like a comic, the app lets you set your own pace: each question sits in its own panel, over landscapes in an ink-and-watercolor style, and nothing moves on until you do.
